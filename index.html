Gameplay basics

Drag your finger to steer your ship. Your weapons fire automatically. Defeat enemy waves, collect coins, and choose an upgrade after each wave. Survive boss encounters and unlock cosmetic ships in the Ship Hangar.

Purchases and restoring access

If you've purchased the optional Supporter Pack, use the game's Restore Purchases option while signed into the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If your purchase doesn't appear, email support with the issue. Do not share your Apple Account password.

Purchases and refunds are processed by Apple. You can also request a refund through Apple.
