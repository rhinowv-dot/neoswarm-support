Neon Swarm: Survival — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Neon Swarm: Survival is a mobile arcade game developed by Adam Rhinehart.

Game data

The game saves gameplay-related information, such as scores, achievements, and unlocked ships, on your device to support gameplay and progression.

In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payments under its own policies. Purchase entitlement information may be used to unlock purchased content or restore access.

Contacting support

If you email us for help, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and the details of your support request. We use that information to respond to your request.

Advertising and analytics

This version of the policy assumes the released game does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If those features are added, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must be updated before release.

Children

The game does not require users to create an account. Do not send personal or sensitive information in support messages.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the game or applicable requirements, with the updated policy posted here.
